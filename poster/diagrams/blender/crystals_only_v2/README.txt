EDITABLE ELECTRIDE CONNECTIVITY FIGURES — CRYSTALS ONLY, V2

Open electride_crystals_v2.blend in Blender.
The saved scene opens on the four-crystal camera view with a transparent background.
There are no headings, material names, panels, or other labels.

Exports
- electride_crystals_v2_preview.png: white-background preview, left to right 0D, 1D, 2D, 3D.
- electride_crystals_v2_transparent.png: the same strip with transparency.
- electride_0d_v2_transparent.png through electride_3d_v2_transparent.png:
  separate 1600 x 1600 transparent PNGs for poster layout.

Editing
- Expand a dimensionality collection to find Host framework and Electron regions.
- Use the named root empty to move or rotate a whole crystal.
- Edit the 2D sheet meshes and their Sheet thickness modifiers to adjust the waves and thickness.
- The 3D network is one connected, directly editable curved mesh.
- The camera and studio lights are in their own collection.

Scientific scope
These are conceptual connectivity schematics with a shared illustrative host framework.
They are not crystallographic reconstructions or calculated ELF/charge-density isosurfaces.
The curved 3D surface was visually informed by the Ba3N partial-charge-density isosurfaces
in ACS Omega Figure 4, DOI 10.1021/acsomega.2c00956, supplied in the reference PowerPoint.
It is not a reproduction of that paper's underlying density data.
