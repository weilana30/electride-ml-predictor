## Core structure-identification rule

Identify the exact structure associated with the electride report in the existing row.

A structure may be assigned to the row only if:

- the primary paper explicitly identifies that structure;
- the paper provides a CIF, atomic coordinates, crystallographic database identifier, or sufficient crystallographic information to reconstruct it; or
- the structure is unambiguously derivable from the paper and there are no competing polymorphs or phases that could fit the description.

Do not assign a structure solely because a Materials Project or other database entry has the same:

- chemical formula;
- reduced formula;
- space group;
- number of atoms;
- approximate lattice parameters; or
- structural prototype.

Database matches may be recorded separately as possible candidates, but they must not be presented as the literature-confirmed structure.

## Information to add

For each existing CSV row, search the primary literature and add the following where available:

### Structure

- exact structure used or discussed in the electride study;
- structure source;
- crystallographic database identifiers;
- Materials Project ID, only if explicitly linked to the paper or unambiguously verified;
- CIF availability and URL;
- space-group symbol and number;
- lattice parameters;
- unit-cell volume;
- atoms per unit cell;
- formula units per unit cell;
- Wyckoff positions;
- structural prototype;
- inorganic-framework dimensionality;
- whether the structure is experimental, relaxed computationally, idealized, or hypothetical;
- whether multiple polymorphs or pressure phases are reported.

### Experimental and computational conditions

- pressure;
- pressure units;
- temperature;
- temperature units;
- synthesis conditions;
- whether the material is stable at ambient pressure;
- whether it is stable at room temperature;
- whether it is recovered after pressure release;
- phase-transition information;
- whether the structure was measured experimentally or generated computationally.

Do not assume ambient pressure or room-temperature stability if it is not reported.

### Electride evidence

- existing classification supported by the literature;
- electride dimensionality: 0D, 1D, 2D, 3D, or mixed;
- location of interstitial electrons;
- whether the electron is described as an anionic electron, interstitial electron, electride electron, electron pair, or similar;
- whether ELF was used;
- whether electron density, Bader/QTAIM, DOS, band structure, magnetic, optical, or transport analysis was used;
- numerical electronic descriptors, if explicitly reported;
- whether the evidence is experimental, theoretical, indirect, direct, qualitative, or quantitative.

Do not convert a predicted or suggested electride into a confirmed electride.

## Structure-confidence labels
